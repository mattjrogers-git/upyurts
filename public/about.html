About UP Yurts

Owner and operator Matt Rogers has more than 25 years of experience in woodworking and building yurts.

With the world's only metal yurt roof replacement system, he's helping yurts live longer, better lives and protecting their owners' investments.

UP Yurts is based in New Paltz, New York, in the Hudson Valley.
