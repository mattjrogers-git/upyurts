Features

Built like nothing else on a yurt

Steel panels, precision-formed, in a size for every yurt and round house.

Solid steel construction

Thick 24-gauge galvanized steel is CNC-folded into the pie-shaped panels that make up each roof.

The high-strength steel is coated to the G-115 specification, a zinc coating nearly 60% thicker than most other metal roofs. That means a longer life and less maintenance.

Bolt-together or screw-down installation

Bolt-together roofs are for new construction, or structures with bare rafters where you can reach the panels from inside. Every panel has pre-punched holes, only a few fastener sizes are needed, and the only tools are a drill, a ½-inch socket or wrench, and a ¼-inch punch or rod to line up the holes.

Screw-down roofs are for fabric yurts with an existing roof cover. They fasten with standard metal roofing screws driven through the cover into the rafters.

The average roof installs in 1 to 3 days, depending on your crew and the roof size.

A range of sizes

Roofs come in 3-foot steps from 12' to 42': 12', 15', 18', 21', 24', 27', 30', 33', 36', 39' and 42'.

Each roof is 3 to 5 inches larger than its stated size, so a 30' roof fits a 30' yurt. When the sizes match exactly, we recommend going up one size.

Larger and smaller roofs are available on request. For round-house builds (not yurt replacements) we offer higher snow-load ratings, roof hatches, covered vents and ladder rungs.

Fits every yurt with a 30° roof pitch

If your yurt has a 30-degree roof pitch, the roof will fit. Most major yurt makers use this angle.

Any protractor, angle finder or free level app on your phone will measure your rafters. Take several readings and average them.
