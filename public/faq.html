FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What sizes do you offer?

Our metal roofs come in 11 sizes: 12', 15', 18', 21', 24', 27', 30', 33', 36', 39' and 42' diameters. Sizes are nominal; the actual diameter is 3 to 4 inches larger. We recommend going up one size for a generous 18-inch eave overhang. See prices.

How do I know if it will fit my yurt?

Two measurements. First and most important is your roof angle: our roofs are made to a 30° pitch to match most major yurt makers, with a 1–2° tolerance. Second is your yurt's diameter.

What are the main benefits of a metal roof over a fabric roof?

  • A wide overhang keeps your walls dry, so windows can stay open in a light rain.
  • Thick steel protects you from falling branches and hail.
  • Our roofs last an average of 70 years, a 75% or greater savings over the life of the roof.
  • Galvanized steel contains only three elements: iron, carbon and zinc.

How will it work with my clear dome?

Our 12' and 15' roofs have a 22-inch center opening. All sizes 18' and larger have a factory-finished 36-inch peak opening. An optional 60-inch dome flashing kit is available for $150. We'll also try to work with your existing dome and hardware, or provide a replacement dome.

How long does it take to install?

Pre-punched bolt holes and fully formed flashing panels make the install quick. A competent crew can install a smaller roof in a day; larger roofs take 1 to 3 days depending on crew size and speed.

How much does the roof weigh? Can my yurt take the weight?

Our roofs weigh about 2 to 2.5 pounds per square foot. Most yurt roofs are rated for 20 to 40 pounds per square foot or more, so this matters only if your yurt's rating is close to your local snow load. When engineered into a structure, the roof is designed for a 22 psf overall load; it doesn't increase your yurt's load capacity. Roof stiffeners and metal rafters are available for round houses.

What are the roof panels made of?

High-quality galvanized steel with a G-115 coating (minimum 350 g/m² of zinc). No other roofing uses a thicker zinc coating, and zinc is what keeps steel from corroding.

Do your roofs fit round buildings other than yurts?

Yes. Our roofs have been used on strawbale, earthbag and wood-frame round houses. The roof can be engineered into the structure as a complete rafter and roof system, or set on standard rafters.

How is my roof shipped?

Shipping is free to your address (some remote locations are excluded; Hawaii and Alaska orders go to a freight forwarder in the lower 48). Your roof arrives as one sturdy wooden crate on an LTL freight truck. You're responsible for unloading, and we're happy to help you plan it.

What countries do you ship to?

We work with international shippers to deliver to Canada, and with logistics companies that deliver to Hawaii and Alaska.

How big and heavy is the crate?

From 6 feet and 850 lb for a 12' roof up to 23½ feet and 4,700 lb for a 42' roof. Weights include about 200 lb of margin. See the crate table for every size.

How do I unload the crate?

12' and 15' crates can come off on the truck's liftgate. 18' and larger roofs need a forklift. Crates over 20 feet (36'–42' roofs) are easiest with fork extensions or two forklifts, so contact us before delivery. Follow our step-by-step unloading guide.

How do I order and pay?

Fill out the order form with your size and delivery address, then pay a 50% deposit by ACH bank debit or check. The balance is due about a week before your roof ships. How to order.
