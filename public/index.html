Replace the roof once

A steel roof that outlasts five fabric covers

Fabric yurt roofs wear out every 10 to 15 years. An UP Yurts metal roof is a one-time investment: heavy galvanized steel panels that shed snow, stop falling branches and hail, and keep your walls dry under a wide overhang.

Screw-down roofs go right over your existing fabric cover, so your yurt is never open to the weather during the install.

Will it fit?

Two measurements tell you

  • Roof pitch: our roofs fit any yurt with a 30° roof pitch, which most major yurt makers use. A level app on your phone will measure your rafters.
  • Diameter: roofs come in 3-foot steps from 12' to 42'. We recommend going up one size for a generous 18-inch overhang.
