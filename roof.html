How to order

  1. Measure your yurt. Confirm a 30° roof pitch and your diameter. Not sure which size? Email us and we'll confirm it with you.
  2. Fill out the order form with your roof size, delivery address and payment choice.
  3. Pay a 50% deposit by ACH bank debit or check. Your roof goes into production once the deposit clears.
  4. Pay the balance about one week before your roof ships. We email you the amount and date first.

Shipping, crate sizes and unloading

Your roof arrives in one sturdy wooden crate on an LTL freight truck. Shipping is free to your address (some remote locations excluded; Hawaii and Alaska orders ship to a freight forwarder in the lower 48). You're responsible for unloading, and we're glad to help you plan it.

Weights include about 200 lb of margin, so plan your equipment for the weight shown. Crate sizes vary by a few inches, and orders with extra parts can ship taller and heavier.

12' and 15' crates come off on the truck's liftgate. 18' and larger need a forklift. Crates over 20 feet (36'–42' roofs) are easiest with fork extensions or two forklifts, so contact us before delivery to plan it.
